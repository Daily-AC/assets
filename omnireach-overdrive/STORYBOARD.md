# Omnireach Overdrive

## Video direction

- Palette system: use the normative `frame.md` tokens. Near-black `white`/`offwhite` surfaces form the canvas; cold `black` is primary ink; `yellow` is the cyan signal; `cream` is the magenta signal; `status-success`, `status-danger`, and `status-warning` are reserved for verified success, actual pain, and caution. Never add another hue.
- Type system: Noto Sans CJK SC display/body and Noto Sans Mono CJK SC technical chrome from local font files. Hero Chinese phrases are short and massive; commands and evidence use stable monospace columns with tabular numerals.
- Motion grammar: every entrance uses an explicit `fromTo` state and a smooth `power3` long-tail settle. Reveal each piece on its Qwen phrase timing, especially through the back half. Fast moves may use a finite motion-blur streak; held content stays still except for bounded low-amplitude jitter.
- Rhythm: Frames 1 and 2 hit hard, Frame 3 becomes one continuous camera traverse, Frame 4 accelerates through three result records, and Frame 5 alternates four proof impacts with a still final CTA. The last 0.25–0.45s of Frames 1–4 is a deliberate held read.
- Surface: hard 4px/3px borders, square corners, offset shadows, scanlines, chromatic text echoes, flat color fields, perspective grids, and deterministic indexed shards. No gradient orbs, glass cards, stock footage, browser screenshots, or real account surfaces.
- Negative motion list: no slideshow front-load, no screensaver drift, no infinite repeat/yoyo, no CSS keyframes/transitions, no random particles, no back-half camera drift, and no mid-frame exit. Between-frame exits belong to the injected transitions.
